How it works

A clear process from first call to completion

You will always know what happens next, what it costs and who is paying us.

30+years of adviser experience in the UK mortgage market
2006INCA Finance established
FCAauthorised, FRN 480650

  1. Initial conversation

    An initial chat at no cost or obligation to understand what you want to achieve. We will explain our services and any fees before you commit.

  2. Fact-find & research

    We look at your income, outgoings and plans, then search the lenders available to us for suitable options.

  3. Recommendation

    We give you a written recommendation and explain why it suits you, along with the costs and risks.

  4. Application to completion

    We submit the application and keep in touch with the lender, valuer, agents and solicitors until you complete.

  5. Ongoing review

    We contact you before your deal ends so you can review your options in good time.
