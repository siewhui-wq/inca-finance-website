Accessibility & extra support

Everyone should be able to get good advice. If you need us to do things differently, just ask.

Extra support

Life events such as illness, bereavement, a relationship breakdown, losing a job or money worries can make financial decisions harder. So can a disability, or English not being your first language. If any of these apply to you, please let us know. We can:

  • give you more time, and arrange shorter or more frequent meetings
  • provide documents in large print or another format
  • include a family member, friend or representative in meetings, with your permission
  • use phone, video or face-to-face meetings, whichever suits you best

Anything you tell us will be handled sensitively and only used to support you.

If you are struggling to pay your mortgage, please speak to your lender as soon as possible. Free, impartial help is also available from MoneyHelper.

Website accessibility

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. It can be used with a keyboard and screen readers, works at up to 400% zoom, and uses colour contrast that meets AA standards. If you have any difficulty using the site, please call us on 0777 9279 021.
