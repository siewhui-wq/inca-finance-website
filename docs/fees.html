Fees & how we're paid

We will always tell you what our service will cost, and agree it with you in writing, before you are committed to anything.

Mortgage advice

Your first meeting with us costs nothing and does not commit you to anything. If you decide to go ahead, we charge a fee for mortgage advice and arranging. The amount depends on how complex your case is.

We will also usually receive commission from the lender. We will tell you how much before you apply, and it will be shown on your mortgage illustration (ESIS).

Insurance

We are normally paid commission by the insurer, which is included in the premium you pay. If we charge a fee instead of, or as well as, commission, we will tell you before you buy.

Your terms of business

Before we provide any service we will give you our Terms of Business. It explains the services we offer, the range of lenders and insurers we consider, our fees and how to complain. You can ask for a copy at any time.
