About us

An established City of London brokerage

INCA Finance was founded in 2006. Our advisers bring more than 30 years' experience in the UK mortgage market.

Our approach

We started INCA Finance to give clients the kind of advice we would want ourselves: honest, thorough and clearly explained. Many of our clients come to us because their situation doesn't fit a lender's standard criteria, such as contractors, company directors, landlords, expats and people buying unusual property.

We work with high-street banks, building societies, specialist lenders and private banks, and we arrange protection and general insurance alongside your mortgage. Our advice is always based on your circumstances and what you want to achieve.
