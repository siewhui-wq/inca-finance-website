Who we are

INCA Finance Ltd (05988620), 2nd Floor, Premier House, 309 Ballards Lane, London N12 8LY, is the controller of the personal data we hold about you. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office under registration number Z1389227. You can contact our Compliance Department about your data at 63 St Mary Axe, London EC3A 8AA or Compliance@incafinance.co.uk.

What we collect

To advise you and arrange mortgages and insurance, we collect:

  • identity and contact details
  • financial information, such as your income, outgoings, assets, debts and credit history
  • employment details and details of the property
  • where needed for protection insurance, health and lifestyle information (special category data)
  • records of our calls and correspondence with you

How we use it and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis
Advising you and arranging mortgages and insuranceContract, or steps before entering a contract
Identity checks, anti-money-laundering checks and keeping regulatory recordsLegal obligation
Health information for protection insuranceYour explicit consent, or insurance purposes under the Data Protection Act 2018
Reviewing your mortgage or cover before it endsLegitimate interests

Who we share it with

We share your information only as needed to provide our service. This may include lenders, insurers, product providers, credit reference and identity-verification agencies, solicitors, valuers and surveyors, our compliance and IT service providers, and regulators and law enforcement where the law requires it. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

We are required to keep records that show why our recommendations were suitable. We keep records of our business transactions indefinitely, and we treat them as confidential even after you stop being a client.

International transfers

If any of our service providers process data outside the UK, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

This website

This website does not use contact forms, analytics or tracking cookies. See our cookie notice.
