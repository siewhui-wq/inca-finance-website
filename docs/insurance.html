Protection

Life & critical illness cover

Protection policies can pay off your mortgage or give your family an income if you die, are diagnosed with a serious illness or cannot work.

  • Life insurance pays a lump sum or regular income if you die during the policy term.
  • Critical illness cover pays a lump sum if you are diagnosed with one of the specified conditions listed in the policy.
  • Income protection pays a monthly benefit if illness or injury stops you working.
ImportantThese policies have no cash-in value at any time. If you stop paying premiums, your cover will end. Each policy pays out only for the events and conditions it lists, so please read the policy terms carefully. You must answer the insurer's questions fully and honestly. If you don't, a claim may not be paid.
General insurance

Home insurance

Most lenders require buildings insurance from the date you exchange contracts. We arrange buildings and contents cover, including accidental damage and high-value items, from a limited number of insurers.

General insurance

Landlord insurance

Standard home insurance does not usually cover a let property. Landlord policies can cover buildings, landlord's contents, loss of rent, legal expenses and property owner's liability, for single lets, HMOs and portfolios.

Commercial

Business & commercial insurance

Cover for commercial and mixed-use property, plus business protection such as key person and shareholder protection, to keep your business stable if something happens to someone it depends on.

Transparency

How we are paid for insurance

We act on your behalf when arranging insurance. We do not charge you a fee for insurance. We are paid commission by the insurer, which is included in the premium you pay. Before you buy any policy, we will tell you whether we are giving advice or information only, and the range of insurers we have considered.

We may be able to place general insurance with a limited number of insurers. If so, we will tell you, and we can give you a list of the insurers we use on request.
