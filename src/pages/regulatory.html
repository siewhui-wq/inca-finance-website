Our permissions

We are authorised and regulated by the FCA for residential mortgages, pure protection and general insurance business. We provide an advised service only; we do not offer an execution-only service.

Unregulated business

Some of the products we arrange are not regulated by the FCA. These include most buy-to-let mortgages, commercial mortgages and some bridging and development finance. For unregulated products you may not have access to the Financial Ombudsman Service or the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. We will always tell you when a product is unregulated.

Financial Ombudsman Service

If you are not happy with how we have handled a complaint, you may be able to refer it to the Financial Ombudsman Service. See our complaints procedure.

Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS)

We are covered by the FSCS. You may be entitled to compensation from the scheme if we cannot meet our obligations. This depends on the type of business and the circumstances of the claim.

  • Mortgage advising and arranging: covered up to £85,000.
  • Insurance advising and arranging: covered for 90% of the claim with no upper limit. Compulsory insurance and long-term insurance, such as life cover, is covered for 100% of the claim.

Further information is available at www.fscs.org.uk or on 0800 678 1100.
