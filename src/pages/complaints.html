We aim to provide an excellent service. If something has gone wrong, please tell us so we can put it right.

What happens next

  1. We will acknowledge your complaint promptly and tell you who is dealing with it.
  2. If we can resolve your complaint by the end of the third business day after we receive it, we will send you a written summary resolution communication confirming this.
  3. Otherwise, we will investigate and keep you informed of our progress. We will send you a final response within eight weeks of receiving your complaint.
  4. If we cannot give you a final response within eight weeks, we will write to explain why, tell you when we expect to respond, and let you know that you can refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

Financial Ombudsman Service

If you are unhappy with our final response, or we have not responded within eight weeks, you may be able to refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service. Their service is free to consumers. You must normally contact them within six months of the date of our final response.
