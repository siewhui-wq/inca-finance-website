Fees & how we're paid

We will always tell you what our service will cost, and confirm it in writing, before you are committed to anything.

Mortgage advice

Your initial consultation is free and does not commit you to anything.

If you decide to go ahead with a mortgage application, we charge a fee for our advice. The amount depends on the type of mortgage and how complex your case is. Part of the fee is payable after your initial consultation and the rest when your mortgage offer is issued. We will explain our fee, when it is payable and when it can be refunded during your initial consultation. You will receive these details in our Terms of Business before you agree to proceed.

We are also paid commission by the lender when your mortgage completes. We will confirm the amount in your mortgage illustration, and we can give you details of the range of commission we receive from lenders on request.

Insurance

We do not charge a fee for advising on or arranging protection or home insurance. We are paid commission by the insurer, which is included in the premium you pay.

Fair value

In line with the FCA's Consumer Duty, we regularly check that our fees and commission represent fair value for the service you receive. A summary of our approach is available on request.

Our Terms of Business

Our Terms of Business explain our services, the lenders and insurers we use, our fees, refunds and how to complain. You can ask for a copy at any time, including in Easy Read or large print.
